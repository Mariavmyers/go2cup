US, Canadian & Australian fans: you need an e-visa

Brazil ended the visa waiver on April 10, 2025. Apply online only at the official portal, brazil.vfsevisa.com. The fee was US$80.90 as of October 2026, processing can take up to 10 business days, and the portal advises applying about two months before you travel. Fees and rules can change — always check the official site.

Questions fans ask

Brazil 2027 FAQ

When does the Women's World Cup 2027 start and end?

It runs from June 24 to July 25, 2027. Brazil play the opening match at the Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro, and the final is at the same stadium.

Which cities host matches?

Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Belo Horizonte, Fortaleza, Brasília, Porto Alegre, Recife and Salvador. Rio and São Paulo host the semi-finals; São Paulo also hosts the 3rd-place match.

Where do I buy tickets?

Only from FIFA at FIFA.com/tickets. As of October 2026 FIFA had not announced sale dates. Avoid resale sites and social-media sellers.

Do I need a visa?

US, Canadian and Australian citizens need a Brazilian e-visa (since April 10, 2025). Many other passports are visa-free. Check the official portal and your government's travel advice before you book.

What's the weather like in June and July?

It's winter. Porto Alegre can be cold and wet; São Paulo, Belo Horizonte and Brasília have cool nights; Rio is mild; Fortaleza, Recife and Salvador stay warm, with rain showers more common in Recife and Salvador.
