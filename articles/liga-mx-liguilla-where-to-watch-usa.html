Where to Watch the Liga MX Liguilla in the USA (Apertura 2026)

Channels, apps and dates for the quarterfinals, semifinals and final, from the US.

SportDGX Daily · November 24, 2026

Quick answer: there's no single channel for the Liguilla in the US. It depends on the home team. Most games air on TUDN, Univision, UniMás and ViX. Chivas home games air on Telemundo and Peacock. Monterrey and Santos home games air on FOX channels. For English commentary, some games air on CBS Sports Network and Paramount+.

Liguilla key dates

  • No Play-In: the regular season ended Nov. 22 (Matchday 17) and the top 8 went straight to the quarterfinals.
  • Quarterfinals: first legs Nov. 25–26, second legs Nov. 28–29.
  • Semifinals: first legs Dec. 2–3, second legs that weekend.
  • Final: Dec. 10 and 13. Heads up: if Toluca makes the final, it moves to Dec. 24 and 27 because of the Intercontinental Cup.

Dates and kickoff times can change. Check the official schedule on game day.

How to watch without cable

  • Free with an antenna: Univision, UniMás, Telemundo and FOX are over-the-air channels, so some games are free with a TV antenna.
  • ViX Premium: TelevisaUnivision's app, with most games.
  • Peacock: for Chivas home games.
  • Live TV streaming: DirecTV Stream includes Univision, TUDN and FOX. Fubo no longer carries Univision, UniMás or TUDN, so it misses many games.
  • Check current prices at sign-up; many services offer a free trial.
