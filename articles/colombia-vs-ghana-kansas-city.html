VS

Colombia vs Ghana

The knockout rounds hit Arrowhead. Here is your last-minute game plan — where to sleep, how to move, and where to celebrate in Kansas City.

Kickoff: Fri, July 3 · 8:30 PM CT
Venue: Kansas City Stadium (Arrowhead)
TV: FOX (EN) · Telemundo (ES)

Matchday Game Plan

  • Arrive 2–3 hours early. Security lines at Kansas City Stadium have been long all tournament; parking lots open well before kickoff.
  • Drive or pre-book a transfer. The stadium is roughly 35–45 minutes from KCI Airport and 20 minutes from downtown. Rideshare surge after the final whistle is brutal.
  • Bag policy is strict. Clear bags only — leave backpacks at the hotel.
  • Hydrate. July in Missouri is hot and humid, and an 8:30 PM kickoff means a long day of tailgating.
  • Cash-free stadium. Cards and mobile pay only inside Arrowhead.

No Tickets? Watch Free

Quick Answers

What time is Colombia vs Ghana in Kansas City?

Kickoff is Friday, July 3, 2026 at 8:30 PM Central Time at Kansas City Stadium (Arrowhead Stadium), Missouri.

Where can I watch if I don't have tickets?

Live on FOX (English) and Telemundo (Spanish), plus free public watch parties at the FIFA Fan Festival and soccer bars across the KC metro.

Are hotels near the stadium still available?

Rooms near the Truman Sports Complex go first. Widen your search to downtown KC, the Plaza, Independence, and Overland Park — all within a 20–30 minute drive.

How do I get from KCI Airport to the stadium?

About 35–45 minutes by car. Book a rental or transfer in advance — matchday rideshare pricing surges hard after the final whistle.
